Since 1947

Seventy-nine years on the record

Founded January 22, 1947. For nearly eight decades the Chamber has supported local businesses, strengthened civic partnerships, and helped connect people to the resources that move our community forward.

Today it offers 3 membership levels, at $275, $1,750 and $200 a year, and keeps office hours 5 days a week. Looking ahead, we remain focused on growth, collaboration, and a stronger future for Greater North Miami.
