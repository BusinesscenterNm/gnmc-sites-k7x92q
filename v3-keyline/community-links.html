Community links

A starting point for useful public and community resources serving North Miami, Miami-Dade County, and South Florida.

Office: 13100 West Dixie Highway, North Miami, FL 33161. Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

A larger network

Greater North Miami is part of a larger Miami-Dade network shaped by local governments, economic development partners, schools, cultural institutions, transportation systems, tourism organizations, and community-serving agencies. These connections help businesses access information, navigate public resources, build partnerships, and stay engaged with the region's growth. Additional links and partner resources will be added over time.
