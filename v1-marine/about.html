About the Chamber

A legacy of connection, advocacy, and growth

One of the original chambers in Northeast Miami-Dade, founded in 1947 and serving businesses across the county today.

Founded January 22, 1947. One of the original chambers in Northeast Miami-Dade.

Founded in 1947

The Greater North Miami Chamber of Commerce was founded in 1947 and is one of the original chambers in Northeast Miami-Dade. Today the Chamber serves businesses across Miami-Dade County, strengthening the business environment, connecting leaders across sectors and municipalities, and helping our region thrive as a place to build, invest, and belong.

What we do

The Chamber hosts networking gatherings, workshops, town halls, and community events, and partners with local government on business development. Through these efforts, we create meaningful opportunities for members to increase visibility, build relationships, and stay connected to the issues shaping the local economy.

A trusted connector

As Greater North Miami continues to grow, the Chamber remains a trusted advocate, resource, and connector for businesses, entrepreneurs, civic partners, and community leaders.
