What you get

Membership benefits

A stronger presence in the business community, and the relationships, resources, and opportunities that support growth.

Office: 13100 West Dixie Highway, North Miami, FL 33161. Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

More than a directory listing

Businesses today need more than a directory listing. They need connection, visibility, and advocacy, and that is exactly what Chamber membership delivers. The Greater North Miami Chamber of Commerce gives members a stronger presence in the business community while helping them build relationships, access resources, and participate in opportunities that support growth across Miami-Dade County.

Eight benefits

What membership delivers

Visibility

Stay visible in a competitive regional market through Chamber communications, member highlights, referrals, sponsorship opportunities, event promotion, and placement within the Chamber's business network.

Networking

Connect through networking gatherings, workshops, town halls, business programs, and community events. Build relationships, exchange ideas, meet potential partners.

Credibility

Membership shows that your business is engaged, connected, and invested in the community it serves, aligning you with a trusted local business network.

Partnerships

Get connected with business owners, nonprofit organizations, civic leaders, educational partners, local government, and regional decision-makers.

Resources

Practical resources, information, programs, and support, from educational workshops and business development opportunities to referrals and community updates.

Involvement

Participate in committees, support Chamber initiatives, contribute ideas, volunteer, sponsor programs, and take on leadership opportunities.

Member perks

When available, exclusive tools, special programs, business discounts, promotional opportunities, and member-only resources.

Advocacy

A stronger presence in the business community and a voice in the conversations shaping Greater North Miami and Miami-Dade.
