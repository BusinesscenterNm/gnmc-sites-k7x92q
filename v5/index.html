Since 1947

Seventy-nine years on the record

Founded January 22, 1947. For nearly eight decades the Chamber has supported local businesses, strengthened civic partnerships, and helped connect people to the resources that move our community forward. Today it offers 3 membership levels, at $275, $1,750 and $200 a year, and keeps office hours 5 days a week. Looking ahead, we remain focused on growth, collaboration, and a stronger future for Greater North Miami.

Why the Chamber matters

A trusted connector for a multicultural business community

We empower businesses, entrepreneurs, and residents through advocacy, education, strategic partnerships, and business support services. From networking and visibility to workforce development and financial literacy, the Chamber strengthens opportunity throughout Miami-Dade County. We also serve as a trusted connector for Greater North Miami's diverse business community, helping local leaders build relationships, share resources, and create lasting economic impact.

The Greater North Miami Chamber of Commerce empowers businesses, entrepreneurs, and residents by fostering economic growth, workforce development, financial literacy, and community revitalization.
